# Catalog evidence

Checked: 2026-10-25. Costs are community reports and must be checked against the current game before spending resources. No claim of a complete in-game catalog or image collection is made.

The initial catalog contains 38 entries: 16 upgrade rows and 22 other recipe rows. Twenty-five entries are costed; 13 are disabled. Nine categories have selectable costed recipes; the Ingredients category initially supports manual collection targets while its two printing entries remain disabled. Expanded factual cross-checking is in progress.

The reference sources distinguish scopes differently: COD Central reports 107 printer cards including 12 upgrades; DMZ Codex lists 95 recipes separately from upgrade families/rewards; other sites publish different category counts. These are source observations, not authoritative game totals.

## References

- [COD Central recipe reference](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-3d-printer-recipes/), updated 2026-10-22.
- [DMZ Codex recipes](https://dmzcodex.com/recipes/) and [upgrade reference](https://dmzcodex.com/upgrades/), checked 2026-10-25.

Only factual names and quantities are represented in the application, with source/status information. Source descriptions, site designs and game art are not included. Restricted collections from DMZTracker and DMZHub are not copied. Newly reviewed Destructoid and OGHub reuse restrictions exclude those sites from further bulk transcription.

Known initial gaps include later upgrade tiers, most equipment recipes, ingredient-printing recipes and a conflicting Radiation Blockers cost. Unknown costs stay disabled rather than being treated as zero.

## Images

The public source contains no game images. The private prototype's original five PNGs have separate provenance and do not establish a public redistribution license. The build detects optional user-supplied artwork locally; absent images use explicit placeholders. See [artwork setup](assets/README.md). Catalog coverage and image coverage are tracked separately.
