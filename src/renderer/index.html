About this prototype

The printer catalog includes crafting recipes and reported upgrade tiers, plus owner-confirmed field box identities with separately labeled requirement evidence. The blueprint and item library adds sourced weapon-manual and cosmetic-blueprint records with separate acquisition routes. Season 1 research was checked on 6 Nov 2026. Full in-game completeness is not claimed; missing requirements stay unknown. Consult each record for source and verification date.

Have it and Want it are manual item records. Wanted library requests appear in a separate Raid section; they do not add crafting ingredients or change physical pickup quantities. Existing printer goals remain in Plan your raid. The app does not read your inventory, grant unlocks or buy anything in game. Older found/unlocked notes are retained in backups without inferring that you physically have an item.

Entries still disabled for unresolved requirements: . Community-reported selectable recipes are labeled and remain unconfirmed in game. Permanent tiers count once; one tier per family can be planned. Earlier tiers must already be owned. Only explicitly selected ingredient printing offsets matching outputs; no automatic recursive expansion or unlock is assumed.

Artwork is optional and is never downloaded by the app. The private starter supplied CPU, Detonators, Nylon Cloth, Power Regulator and Reactive Metals images via DMZ Codex. Game art belongs to Activision/respective rights holders; no public redistribution license is asserted. The library includes shared item-class icons and unmodified official promotional panels, including some variant or bundle panels. Visible captions identify these images, and the enlarged preview shows source URLs and rights notices. All third-party artwork remains copyright reserved; no reuse or redistribution license is asserted. Military Composites uses a non-destructive crop of an owner screenshot; Foreign Intelligence Targets also uses an icon-only crop of an owner gameplay screenshot. Five field box icons use SVG viewport crops of one unchanged owner screenshot; their surrounding labels, locks and inventory counts are hidden in every view, including the enlarged preview. Missing pictures use neutral labeled placeholders.
