About this prototype

The original two-upgrade picture-first sample is preserved. The catalog includes the 95 recipes in two compared public references and 25 reported upgrade tiers; full in-game completeness is not claimed. Requirements were compared on 25 Oct 2026; check your current in-game card before planning.

Seven entries remain disabled for unresolved requirements: Radiation Blockers, DDOS, Stash Item IV, Stash Weapon III, MIA II–IV and Vendor I–II. Permanent tiers count once; one tier per family can be planned. Earlier tiers must already be owned. Only explicitly selected ingredient printing offsets matching outputs; no automatic recursive expansion or unlock is assumed.

Artwork is optional and is never downloaded by the app. The private starter supplied CPU, Detonators, Nylon Cloth, Power Regulator and Reactive Metals images via DMZ Codex. Game art belongs to Activision/respective rights holders; no public redistribution license is asserted. The source omits the item-image collection; documentation previews may contain third-party game art. Military Composites uses a non-destructive crop of an owner screenshot; Foreign Intelligence Targets picture remains pending. Missing pictures use neutral labeled placeholders.
