The artwork-inclusive Windows prerelease bundles 130 image files: 127 source-attributed files from [DMZ Codex](https://dmzcodex.com/) item, recipe and upgrade pages, and three owner-supplied gameplay screenshots for Regional Combat Notes, Military Composites and Foreign Intelligence Targets. The original screenshots are preserved; CSS presentation shows only the relevant item regions. The earlier incorrect Military Composites reference is retained as an unused source file. Five of the DMZ Codex-attributed files were supplied in the original starter bundle. Some item variants share a source image; file counts are not counts of unique game items or tiers. All41 current loot-target names have mapped local images; this is not a claim of full game coverage or unique artwork for every upgrade tier.

DMZ Codex is credited as the reference/image source, not as the owner of Activision's game art. See its [About and attribution page](https://dmzcodex.com/about/), [item references](https://dmzcodex.com/items/), [recipes](https://dmzcodex.com/recipes/), and [upgrade references](https://dmzcodex.com/upgrades/). The accompanying ARTWORK-SOURCES.json records each bundled filename, source page/image URL where available, checked date and SHA256. The owner-supplied gameplay screenshots have no claimed third-party source-site URLs.
