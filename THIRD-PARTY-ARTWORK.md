The Windows test candidate bundles 174 image files. The previous 130 files remain unchanged: 127 source-attributed DMZ Codex files and three owner-supplied gameplay screenshots for Regional Combat Notes, Military Composites and Foreign Intelligence Targets. Original owner screenshots remain unchanged and use their existing CSS icon crops. Some tiers share art, and an earlier unused Military Composites reference is retained.

The new 44 files are two shared game icons referenced by DMZ Codex and 42 complete official Call of Duty promotional panels. These map to 94 new records: 34 Weapon Manuals, 59 cosmetic blueprints and Staff Emergency Exfil Keycard. The Manual/key icons are explicitly shared images, not unique pictures of every entry. Some promotional panels show multiple variants, bundle contents or rewards; the app labels their scope and provides a full-image preview. Fifteen other X01 entries retain placeholders. New image bytes are not cropped, altered or generated.

Game artwork is credited to Activision/respective rights holders. DMZ Codex is credited as the reference/image source, not asserted to own Activision art. Official panels are credited to Activision / Call of Duty and their exact article URLs. ARTWORK-SOURCES.json records each bundled filename, source page, image URL where available, credit, check date and SHA256. IMAGE-SOURCES-2026-11-06.json records new file-to-entry mappings and remaining placeholders.

No affirmative artwork-use or redistribution license was established. The owner requested this credited artwork test release; that request, attribution, public availability, crawling guidance and prior release inclusion do not establish a rights-holder license or endorsement. This notice grants no downstream rights to copy, extract, modify or redistribute third-party artwork. COD Brand Assets terms were not extended to arbitrary item icons or screenshots.

MIT covers original application code only. Third-party game art, trademarks, supplied gameplay images and documentation screenshots containing them are excluded. Individual game images and owner runtime data remain excluded from the public source tree. An artwork-free build is available through package:public. No images are fetched at runtime.
